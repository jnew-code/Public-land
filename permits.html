Permits explained

Rules change every season. Verify with TPWD. Not legal advice.

Short version of TPWD's rules for 2026-27, with the TPWD page for each. Checked Nov 18, 2026.

APH: Annual Public Hunting permit, $48

  • Full privileges on APH areas: hunting, fishing, camping, hiking and other recreation.
  • You must hold or buy a season hunting license to buy it. Required stamps and endorsements still apply.
  • Youth under 17 may access these areas free with a permitted adult.
  • Valid Sept. 1, 2026 to Aug. 31, 2027. Sold at TPWD offices, license dealers, 1-800-TX-LIC-4U and online.

LPU: Limited Public Use permit, $12

  • Same areas as APH for non-hunting use: fishing, hiking, camping, birding, picnicking, wildlife viewing, and horses where allowed.
  • No hunting with an LPU.

Drawn hunts

  • Some state parks and WMAs (in this guide: Lake Whitney, Dinosaur Valley, Fort Boggy, Keechi Creek) are hunted only through the Public Hunt Drawing, with limited permits per hunt.
  • You apply online by a deadline. The 2026-27 deadlines for the areas in this guide have passed; check TPWD for next year's.
  • E-Postcard hunts are part of the drawing. Adults must buy an APH permit before they can apply, and all hunters need a hunting license.
  • Each hunt's page lists the number of permits, fees, means and rules.

Regular (daily) permit, $20

  • For certain small game and migratory bird hunts, first come first served.
  • The fee is waived for youth under 17 and for anyone with an APH permit. A hunting license is still required.
  • Check in and out each day at the area check station.

State park entry

Hunters in a drawn state-park hunt follow the hunt's own rules. Everyone else visiting a state park pays that park's entry fee. See the page for each park in the guide.

On-site registration (OSR)

Many APH areas require you to register each day, on site or online (eOSR). TPWD's page links both.

Licenses

APH and LPU do not replace a hunting or fishing license where one is required.
