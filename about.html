About this guide

Rules change every season. Verify with TPWD. Not legal advice.

This is a free guide to Texas public hunting land in Central Texas (and one example outside the region). It is not affiliated with Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD).

How the data works

  • Every fact links to the TPWD page it came from (the numbered sources at the bottom of each area).
  • Maps are links to TPWD's own maps. This guide draws no boundaries and drops no pins.
  • Where TPWD pages disagree, the page shows both and marks it CONFLICT. It does not pick a winner.
  • Anything not checked yet shows PLACEHOLDER: awaiting data. Do not treat it as fact. "Not stated in official sources" means the TPWD pages checked do not say.
  • Seasons shown are for the season named on each page (2026-27 now). Long official hunt-table text is shortened to an excerpt with a link to the full TPWD listing. If a page's last-verified season is not the current one, it says so.
  • Annual APH lease units (small walk-in dove and small-game tracts) can change from year to year. A unit that was open last season may not be in the current booklet.

What “Central Texas” means here

Counties included: Bell, Blanco, Bosque, Brazos, Brown, Burleson, Burnet, Caldwell, Coleman, Comal, Comanche, Coryell, Erath, Falls, Fayette, Freestone, Gillespie, Grimes, Hamilton, Hays, Hill, Hood, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble, Lampasas, Lee, Leon, Limestone, Llano, Madison, Mason, McCulloch, McLennan, Milam, Mills, Navarro, Palo Pinto, Robertson, San Saba, Somervell, Travis, Washington and Williamson. That is meant to cover the Cross Timbers, Blackland Prairie, Post Oak Savannah and eastern Edwards Plateau / Hill Country around Waco, Temple/Killeen, Austin, Bryan-College Station, Clifton/Stephenville, Brownwood and San Saba/Llano. Kimble County is included so South Llano River State Park is not left out. J.D. Murphree WMA is kept only as an outside-region example.

Areas listed are ones found in TPWD’s 2026-27 APH data and drawn-hunt catalog (plus official WMA and state park hunt pages). A “Last verified” date of Nov. 19, 2026 means the facts on that page were checked against those sources on that day. Older pages that were not re-opened still show Nov. 18, 2026.
